Let's colour!
WHY COLOURING

When pain is loud, give it less of your attention.

Flare-ups can make it hard to think about anything else. Colouring in is a small, low-effort way to shift your focus for a while: no screen to scroll, no decisions to make, just colours and shapes.

Somewhere else for your mind to go

Choosing colours and filling in shapes gives your attention something gentle to hold on to while you rest.

No skill, no pressure

The outlines are already drawn. Grab whatever pencils or crayons are nearby and stop whenever you need to.

Something to share with the kids

A calm activity for a rest day. Pick their favourite movie, print a few pages and colour side by side.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Everything you need for a colouring break.

Colour Quest finds colouring pages from across the web and makes them easy to print, keep and share around the family.

Search any movie

Type a movie or show, or tap a favourite. Colour Quest finds clean black-and-white line drawings first.

Print in one tap

Every picture is sized to fill a single page, on A4 or Letter, with nothing extra around it.

Favourites

Star the pictures you love to keep them close, then download them all at once as a zip.

A profile for each child

Each child picks a name and a friendly avatar, with their own movie buttons and favourites.

Syncs with your WellNest account

Profiles, movies and favourites follow you to any device where you sign in to WellNest.

Credits the creators

Each picture shows the website it came from, and the artist's name when that website lists one.

HOW IT WORKS

Three taps to colouring.

  1. Pick a movie

    Type a name or tap one of your movie buttons.

  2. Choose a picture

    Look closer, star your favourites or download a few.

  3. Print and colour

    Tap Print, grab your pencils and take a break.

QUESTIONS

Good to know

Who can use Colour Quest?

Colour Quest is a bonus for WellNest Premium members, including anyone on the 30-day free trial. Sign in with your WellNest account to start.

Where do the pictures come from?

Colour Quest is a search tool. The colouring pages are found on and loaded from other websites. Colour Quest doesn't host, own or create them, and all characters and artwork belong to their respective owners. Pictures are for personal, at-home colouring.

Is it a treatment for pain?

No. Colour Quest is a relaxing activity to help pass the time, not a medical treatment. Keep following the advice of your doctor and care team, and get medical help if your symptoms change or worry you.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. Searches use safe-search filtering, and each child can have their own profile. As with anything online, we recommend a grown-up stays nearby.
