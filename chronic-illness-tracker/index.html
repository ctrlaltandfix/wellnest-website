One chronic illness tracker for asthma, chronic pain and epilepsy

Looking for a chronic health tracker that handles more than one condition? Managing long-term conditions can mean juggling separate notes for pain, peak flow, seizures, medications and appointments. WellNest combines a symptom diary, medication tracker and doctor-ready PDF health summaries in one Australian web and iPhone app, so you can keep a dated health history and prepare information for your care team.

FAQ

Can one app track asthma, chronic pain and epilepsy?

Yes. WellNest includes separate logs for asthma symptoms and peak flow, chronic pain and flares, and seizures, plus shared medication and appointment records.

Can I send a report to my doctor?

Yes. You can export your recorded health history as a PDF and decide what to share with your treating clinician.

Does WellNest detect seizures or asthma attacks?

No. WellNest is a record-keeping app, not a medical device or emergency monitoring service. Follow your clinician's action plan; in an emergency in Australia call 000.

Does WellNest work on an iPhone or a computer?

WellNest is available through a web browser and on iPhone. The site also offers a free no-signup interactive preview.

Is WellNest free?

WellNest is free to start: every account gets 30 days of Premium with no card, and logging stays free after that. Some educational guides and the interactive report preview are free without a subscription.
