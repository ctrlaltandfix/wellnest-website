YOUR DATA, YOUR DECISION

A private place for
what matters most.

Your health story belongs to you. WellNest keeps your records connected to your own account, not visible to other users, and never sells your personal or health information to advertisers or data brokers.

01 / ON THIS DEVICE

Keep it close.
Store locally.

Prefer to keep your records on the device you use? Leave optional cloud backup switched off in Settings. Local records stay on that device rather than being included in that backup.

Your device, your choice
02 / YOUR CLOUD ACCOUNT

Take it with you.
Access remotely.

Turn on cloud backup in Settings to save supported records to your private WellNest account, so you can access them when you sign in on another supported device.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got you.

Curious how it works? Here are a few things worth knowing before you begin.

How we think about privacy
01What can I track with WellNest?
Keep dated records of pain, symptoms, medications, appointments, exercises and medical expenses. Dedicated trackers support chronic pain, CRPS, asthma and epilepsy.
02Can I try WellNest before subscribing?
Yes. Try the interactive preview without an account, or create an account for 30 days of Premium with no card needed. After the trial you can keep logging on the free plan, or choose Premium.
03What does Premium cost after the free trial?
Premium is A$4.99 per month or A$44.99 per year, billed yearly. You choose whether to subscribe. If you subscribe during your free trial, billing starts when it ends. Plans renew until cancelled; cancel anytime.
04Is WellNest a substitute for medical advice?
No. WellNest is a personal organisation and tracking tool. It does not diagnose conditions or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
