How to choose a chronic health tracker app

By Chris Greenfield, founder of WellNest · Published 11 October 2026

A chronic health tracker helps you record symptoms over time and organise information for appointments. There is no single best app for everyone: the right choice depends on which conditions you live with, what you need to record, your device and how you want to share information.

This guide is published by WellNest, whose product is among the options discussed.

Quick answer: which type of tracker should I choose?

Choose a condition-specific tracker when details such as pain location, asthma peak-flow readings or seizure duration matter to your records. Choose a more flexible general symptom journal when you primarily want custom daily questions for sleep, energy, mood or other symptoms. Before choosing any app, check its platforms, cost, privacy information and whether you can export a report your clinician can actually read.

Questions to ask before signing up

  • What can I record? Check that the symptoms and context important to you are supported.
  • Can I export? Look for an understandable report and control over what gets shared.
  • Does it work on my device? Confirm native apps and browser support.
  • What will I pay? Compare trial terms and ongoing subscription costs on the official pricing pages.
  • How is my information handled? Review the privacy policy and options for accessing or deleting your records.
  • What is the app not for? A record-keeping app should never replace emergency services or clinical guidance.

FAQ

What is a chronic health tracker?

A chronic health tracker is an app or diary for recording ongoing symptoms and related health information over time, such as pain, medication, appointments and daily impact. It is a record-keeping tool, not a diagnosis.

Which app tracks chronic pain, asthma and seizures together?

WellNest offers condition-specific records for chronic pain and CRPS, asthma peak flow and symptoms, and seizure events alongside medications and appointments. Check the official feature pages for current details.
