Built by someone who lives with it

WellNest was built in Australia by Chris Greenfield, who lives with chronic illness and got tired of doctors only ever seeing a snapshot.

Why WellNest exists

Chris lives with chronic illness. Between appointments, his story lived in scattered notes, half-remembered details and a phone gallery of photos that made sense to nobody. His doctors needed to see the complete picture — the flare-ups, the medications, the triggers, the costs — not a two-week recollection squeezed into a 15-minute consult.

So he built WellNest: one calm, organised place to keep the whole story of your health, ready to share with any doctor, any time.

Built in Australia

WellNest is built and supported in Australia by CTRL ALT and FIX (ABN 54 632 545 705). Prices are in Australian dollars, emergency messaging references 000, and the app is designed around Australian privacy expectations for handling sensitive health information.

What WellNest is — and isn't

WellNest is a record-keeping and organisation tool. It does not diagnose, treat, predict or monitor any condition, and it is not a medical device, a diagnostic service or an emergency service. It helps you keep dated, accurate records of your symptoms, medications, appointments and expenses, and share them with your qualified healthcare professionals. In an emergency call 000.
