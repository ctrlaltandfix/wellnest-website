Fibromyalgia rarely looks the same two days in a row. WellNest gives you a quick daily check-in for pain, fatigue, sleep and mood, keeps your medications alongside it, and turns it all into a clear summary for your doctor.

Add fibromyalgia as a condition

WellNest lets you add fibromyalgia as one of your health conditions, next to condition-specific trackers like the chronic pain diary and asthma log.

What you can log each day

An overall severity from 1–10; the symptoms you felt; your mood; energy level; sleep quality; triggers for the day; and free-form notes – a quick daily check-in that takes under a minute.

Medications and appointments in the same place

Keep your medications, doses, refills and specialist appointments next to your symptom logs, so dose changes and flare-ups sit on the same timeline.

Share a clear summary

Export a PDF report of your symptom history and medications for your GP, rheumatologist or pain specialist.

Why tracking helps with fibromyalgia

Fibromyalgia symptoms often move together: a poor night's sleep, a stressful week or an over-busy day can show up as more pain and fatigue a day or two later. A quick daily check-in makes those links visible, which is hard to do from memory when you're already exhausted.

Use your log to pace yourself

Pacing means spreading activity so you avoid the boom-and-bust cycle of overdoing it on a good day and paying for it afterwards. Logging your activity and exercises next to pain, fatigue and sleep helps you see how much is too much, and helps your doctor, physio or psychologist plan with you.

Keep it quick on bad days

A tracker only helps if you keep using it. On a bad day, a pain score, a fatigue level and one line of notes is enough. Consistency over weeks matters far more than detail on any single day.

Can WellNest track fibromyalgia?

Yes. Add fibromyalgia as a condition and log daily severity, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep quality, triggers and notes.
